Privacy Policy

Trusted Test Taker · Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version

Trusted Test Taker does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything stays on your phone.

What the app does with your data

  • Your PDF books are copied into the app's private storage on your device and read there. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your test results and history are saved only on your device.
  • No account is needed, and the app does not ask for your name, email, or contact details.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are built into the app.

Purchases

The one-time "Full Version" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The app only learns whether your Apple ID has bought it. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a book inside the app, or delete the app itself, to remove all of its data from your phone.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
