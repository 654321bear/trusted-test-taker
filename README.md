# trusted-test-taker

## Description
Study smarter with the books you already own. Pick a PDF textbook and practice with its own review questions, chapter by chapter.
Trusted Test Taker turns your PDF textbooks and practice-question books into tests, so you can study with the questions written by the book's authors.


HOW IT WORKS
• Add a PDF from Files, iCloud Drive, or any app that shares PDFs
• The app finds the chapters, review questions, and answer key
• Take a test on one chapter or the whole book
• See your score, the right answers, and the book's explanations
• Retake only the questions you missed

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your iPhone
• No account and no sign-up
• No internet needed after you install the app
• Your books and results never leave your phone

TRY IT FREE
Add one book and take 3 tests for free. Unlock the full version with a single purchase for unlimited books and tests. No subscription.

WORKS BEST WITH
Text-based PDFs that have multiple-choice review questions and an answer key, such as certification study guides and practice-test books. Scanned (image-only) PDFs are not supported yet.
